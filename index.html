About

Bridging the Gap in Welfare Coordination

HumanityLink is proposed by SQL Syndicate at United International University as a comprehensive solution to the scattered and inefficient state of charitable coordination in Bangladesh. We combine food donation, medical welfare reporting, fundraising, and educational assistance into one transparent, trackable platform.
